# Agent instructions — puen-engineer

Purpose: preserve finalized engineering reasoning, decisions, and workflow knowledge.

## Rules
- Separate **proposed**, **accepted**, **superseded**, and **experiment** states.
- Do not turn tentative chat ideas into approved standards without explicit agreement.
- Each major decision needs context, alternatives, trade-offs, decision, and consequences.
- Keep documents self-contained, date decisions, and cross-link related material.
- Prefer concise, actionable documents over chat transcripts and duplicated notes.
- Put executable skills, agents, and automation scripts in `puen-stack`, not here.
- This repository is PUBLIC. Never copy real internal Jira stories, proprietary architectures, source code, secrets, or private user data into it.
- Flag uncertain facts and unverified assumptions clearly.
- Report what changed and any open questions.
